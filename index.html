Training notes

Effort: Leave 1–2 reps in reserve on most sets.

Rest: 2–3 minutes for compound lifts; 60–120 seconds for isolation work.

Progression: Reach the top of the rep range on every set with clean technique, then increase weight slightly.

Shoulder: Use comfortable ranges. Stop movements that reproduce pain; have persistent symptoms assessed by a physiotherapist.
